# Product Discovery Skill

## Purpose
Guide the rigorous extraction of user problems, validation of market need, and firm demarcation of system boundaries. Prevent scope creep by differentiating core MVP capabilities from secondary HRIS/ERP features.

## Discovery Workflow
1. **Problem Space Exploration**: Identify the core pain point. In Pish Hesabdari, this is information asymmetry, unverified candidate credentials, and legal exposure before Labor Dispute Boards.
2. **Boundary Definition Matrix**: For every proposed feature, explicitly classify it:
   - **In-Scope (Core MVP)**: Directly serves pre-hire validation, statutory wage simulation, or document authenticity.
   - **Out-of-Scope (Strict Exclusions)**: General ledger accounting, live bank disbursement, punch-clock hardware, candidate job portals.
   - **Deferred (Phase 2+)**: Post-hire annual salary review optimization, ongoing employee performance reviews, operational exit execution.
3. **User Persona Profiling**: Define exact decision-makers:
   - *CFO*: Total Cost to Employer (CTC) predictability, cash flow reserves.
   - *HR Director*: Legally defensible salary offers, compression avoidance.
   - *Legal Counsel*: Dispute-immune *Hokm-e Kargozini*, compliance with Article 41 & Precedent 272.
4. **Acceptance Gate**: No feature enters engineering without a validated user problem statement and a proven absence of scope creep.
