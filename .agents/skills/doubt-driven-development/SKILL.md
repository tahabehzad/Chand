# Doubt-Driven Development (Adversarial Red-Team) Skill

## Purpose
Systematically hunt for unstated assumptions, temporal hallucinations, legal misinterpretations, and mathematical blindspots. Act as a skeptical defense against erroneous consensus.

## Skeptical Inquiry Checklist
1. **Temporal Reality Check**:
   - What is the current solar date?
   - Has the statutory year for this regulation expired?
   - Are we treating past events as future or future draft laws as binding law?
2. **Statutory Text Cross-Examination**:
   - Does this claim cite the exact Article, Note, Circular, or Court Precedent?
   - Have we read the full original Persian text of the article, including its caveats? (e.g. Article 11 probation termination rules!).
   - Has a higher court (Diwan-e Edalat-e Edari or Supreme Court) overturned this administrative circular?
3. **Mathematical First Principles**:
   - Is there double counting in cost models (e.g. paid leave inside annual salary)?
   - Does the formula handle edge cases (zero variance, negative numbers, extreme inputs)?
4. **Vendor & Licensing Skepticism**:
   - Does this open-source library carry viral copyleft licenses (AGPL)?
   - Can this API be called by a private company under current Iranian government regulations?
